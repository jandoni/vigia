\noindent{\scshape How to read this document}\par\vspace{3pt}
\noindent Every quantitative figure is labelled either \measured{} — produced
by our own evaluation harness, with the dataset and script named — or
\reported{}, published by the people who built the component. The two are
never mixed, and a figure we have not measured is marked as such rather than
omitted. Proportions carry the interval their sample size supports
(\cref{sec:metrics}); several of this project's headline numbers rest on
small samples, and a point estimate alone would overstate them.\par
\vspace{6pt}
\noindent Every quantitative claim traces to a committed measurement file
under \texttt{eval/results/}, produced by a named evaluation script and
checked against the text by an automated guard (\cref{sec:reproducibility}).
Nothing reported here is entered by hand.
\end{minipage}
