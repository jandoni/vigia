\section{Corrections and version note}
\label{sec:changelog}

This is version~\DocVersion{} of the preprint. In keeping with the paper's
commitment to reporting its own errors, the substantive corrections made to
quantitative claims during preparation are recorded here, so that a reader
encountering an earlier draft can see what changed and why.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Fire false-alarm rate.} The motivating figure — that the
        published fire detector alarms on frames containing no smoke — was
        first measured on \FireNarrowNegatives{} negatives and carried a
        standing caveat that it was indicative. Widening the negative set to
        \FireNegatives{} images across 21 cameras moved the image-level
        false-alarm rate from \FireNarrowFAR{} to \FireImageFAR{}; every
        quotation was corrected to the wider figure
        (\cref{sec:fire}, \cref{tab:intervals}).
  \item \textbf{Building-access detector.} An initial detector trained in-house
        reached civilian box recall of $0.141$. It was replaced by the
        dataset authors' published weights (\BuildingBoxRecall) once the
        licence exposure was found to be containable, and the earlier figure
        is reported alongside the replacement rather than removed
        (\cref{sec:building}).
  \item \textbf{Retracted temporal claim.} An earlier draft described the
        building-access footage as a usable time sequence; inspection of the
        authors' raw frames showed 362 scene cuts across 615 frames, and the
        claim was withdrawn (\cref{sec:demo}). The hazard is now presented as
        detector-only (\cref{fig:qual-building}).
  \item \textbf{Suppression headline.} The single ``suppression'' figure was
        decomposed into filtering and deduplication once the two were found to
        differ by a factor of eight between hazards, and the false-alarm claim
        was scoped to the filtering component alone
        (\cref{sec:decomposition}).
  \item \textbf{Threshold-matched control.} The claim that the validator
        reduces false alarms was re-scoped after a control experiment showed
        an oracle-tuned confidence threshold matching its false-alarm rate
        more cheaply on the easy negative set (\cref{sec:threshold-matched}).
\end{itemize}
